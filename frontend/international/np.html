What is KrashiMitra?

KrashiMitra — "Farmer's Friend" — is a free digital farming platform. Originally built for India's Hindi-speaking farmers, Nepal shares the same Devanagari script, making it immediately accessible to every Nepali farmer with zero language barrier.

Crop Advisory in Nepali
Ask about rice blight, potato disease or cardamom cultivation — expert answers in Nepali instantly.
Mountain-Aware Weather
Zone-wise forecasts for Terai, Hills and the High Himalayas — each with different agricultural windows.
Live Market Prices
Crop prices from Asan, Kalimati and local haats — know when to sell for maximum profit.
