What is KrashiMitra?

KrashiMitra — "Farmer's Friend" — is a free digital farming platform originally built for 100M+ Indian farmers. We are now bringing the same tools to Indonesia — an archipelago nation with 40M+ farmers from Aceh to Papua.

Smart Crop Consultant
Ask about rice pests, palm oil fertilization, or plant diseases in Bahasa Indonesia — instant expert answers.
BMKG Weather & Seasonal Calendar
Rain and monsoon forecasts for every province and island group to plan planting precisely.
National Market Prices
Live crop prices from Pasar Induk Kramat Jati and regional markets so you sell at fair rates.
