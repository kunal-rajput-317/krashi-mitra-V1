What is KrashiMitra?

KrashiMitra — "Farmer's Friend" — is a free digital farming platform. Originally built for Indian farmers, we now bring the same tools to Sri Lanka — the Pearl of the Indian Ocean — with its 2 million farming households across rice paddies, tea estates and spice gardens.

Smart Crop Advisory
Ask about rice blast, tea blister blight or coconut mite infestation — expert answers in Sinhala, Tamil or English instantly.
Two-Monsoon Intelligence
Sri Lanka has two monsoons — Southwest (Yala) and Northeast (Maha). Zone-specific farming guidance for both seasons.
Live Market Prices (Dambulla, Colombo)
Crop prices from Dambulla Dedicated Economic Centre, Colombo and local Pola markets.
