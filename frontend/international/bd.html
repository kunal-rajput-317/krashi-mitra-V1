What is KrashiMitra?

KrashiMitra — "Farmer's Friend" — is a free digital farming platform. Originally built for Indian farmers, we now bring the same tools to Bangladesh's 16+ million farming households.

Ask any farming question in Bangla or English and get expert advice instantly — crop diseases, fertilizer dosages and more.
Localized Weather & Flood Alerts
